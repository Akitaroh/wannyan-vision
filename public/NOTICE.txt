わんにゃん視覚 — image transformation references

This application uses a human dichromacy approximation for education.
It is NOT a validated, species-calibrated simulation of dog or cat appearance.

Matrix: Machado, G. M., Oliveira, M. M., Fernandes, L. A. F. (2009).
A Physiologically-based Model for Simulation of Color Vision Deficiency.
IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics 15(6):1291–1298.
https://doi.org/10.1109/TVCG.2009.113

Coefficients cross-checked with DaltonLens-Python (deuteranopia endpoint).
https://github.com/DaltonLens/DaltonLens-Python
